# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to feel safe and worthwhile for everyone, whatever their background, identity, level of experience, or way of contributing. We pledge to keep our community free of harassment, and to act fairly and promptly when something goes wrong.

## How we treat each other

Disagree openly and kindly. Disagreement about ideas is normal and useful here; criticism should be aimed at work, never at the person behind it. Assume good faith, especially from newcomers, people learning in public, and people writing in a second language.

Be constructive. When you point out a problem, say what would improve things. Share what you know generously, credit others for their help, and keep promises you can actually keep.

Respect privacy. People sometimes share personal details while asking for help. Use that information only to help, and do not repeat it elsewhere.

Represent the project honestly. Do not claim affiliations the project does not have, and speak for yourself unless you hold a maintainer role.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not tolerated in any project space:

- Harassment, insults, stalking, or repeated unwanted attention after being asked to stop.
- Slurs or demeaning comments tied to race, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or nationality.
- Sharing someone else's private information without consent, including anything disclosed in project discussions.
- Threats, intimidation, incitement of violence, or encouragement of self-harm.
- Sexualized language, images, or unwelcome attention of a sexual nature.
- Deliberate deception, plagiarism of others' contributions, or sustained bad-faith disruption of discussions.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness behavior covered above, please tell us. Open an issue in this repository, or if you prefer to stay private for now, email the maintainers at <itsirellzane@gmail.com>.

Reports are handled by the project maintainers and treated confidentially among them. We may ask follow-up questions to understand what happened, and we will not disclose your identity or the contents of your report beyond the people needed to resolve it, unless you ask us to or the law requires it.

Reports naming a maintainer are handled by the remaining maintainers instead.

## Enforcement

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately. When a report is upheld, maintainers apply the lightest consequence that fits the facts, with a bias toward correction over punishment:

1. Correction. A private message explaining the violation and inviting better behavior. This suits minor lapses by people acting in good faith.
2. Warning. An explicit notice that further violations lead to exclusion. Suits a repetition of a corrected lapse or carelessness affecting others' safety or dignity.
3. Temporary ban. Exclusion from participating in the project for a stated period. Suits serious or repeated violations.
4. Permanent ban. Removal from all future participation. Reserved for sustained harassment, threats, or refusal to change course after warnings.

We keep a private record of reports and outcomes. Consequences attach to behavior, not to opinions about design, quality, or priorities, and none of the steps above limit anyone's right to fork the code or publish criticism.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every space where the community gathers around this project, including issue trackers, code reviews, mailing lists, chat rooms, online events, and in-person events. It also applies when someone acts on the project's behalf elsewhere, for example by presenting as a maintainer or publishing material that claims project affiliation.
